<summary><b>New York</b></summary>
<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

New York City (NYC) is the most populous city in the United States, serving as a global center for finance, culture, media, and international diplomacy.

### Places to visit in New York

<!-- LEVEL 1 ATTRACTION: STATUE OF LIBERTY -->
<details>
<summary>Statue of Liberty</summary>
<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

The Statue of Liberty (officially named Liberty Enlightening the World) is a colossal neoclassical copper sculpture on Liberty Island in New York Harbor. Given to the United States by the people of France to celebrate the centennial of the American Declaration of Independence and mutual friendship, it was dedicated on October 28, 1886.

<summary>Empire State Building</summary>
<div style="margin-left: 20px;">

The Empire State Building is a 102-story, Art Deco skyscraper located in Midtown Manhattan, New York City. Completed in 1931, it stands as one of the world's most famous architectural icons and a symbol of American engineering.
